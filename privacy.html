Luna Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What we collect

  • When you sign in with Google: your email address, display name and profile picture, used only to create and identify your Luna account.
  • Your Luna data: liked songs, playlists, play counts and recently played songs, so they sync between your devices.

How we use it

Only to run Luna (sign-in and syncing). We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for anything else.

Storage and deletion

Data is stored on the Luna server. To have your account and data deleted, email ContactLunaSupport@gmail.com.

Third parties

Luna uses Google for sign-in. Music search, playback and lyrics come from public services such as YouTube Music and LRCLIB; searches you make may be sent to them.
